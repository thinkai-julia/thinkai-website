Common questions

Questions we hear before the first call.

How is this different from a chatbot or automation tool I could set up myself?

Off-the-shelf tools give you the software; ThinkAI gives you the strategy, the writing, and the ongoing tuning that make that software actually sound like your business and actually convert. A strategist reviews and refines what the AI does, it's never left to run unsupervised on a generic template.

Do I need any technical knowledge to get started?

No. We handle the strategy, the writing, and the technical setup. You tell us about your business; we build the system around it.

How long does it take to launch?

Most systems are live in days, not months, though timelines vary by scope. Full timelines are laid out on a strategy call once we understand what you need.

What if I only need one service, not the whole system?

That's fine. Every service on this site works on its own, and can be added to later as it makes sense for your business.

Is there a contract, or can I cancel anytime?

Terms are discussed and agreed on a strategy call so they fit the scope of your project. Nothing is locked in without a conversation first.

How much does this cost?

It depends on which services and how much scope your business needs. Book a strategy call for a real number, not a generic price list.
