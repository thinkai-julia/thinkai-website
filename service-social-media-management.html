Why this matters

A brand that goes quiet for a week reads as a brand that's stopped paying attention.

Social media is judged on consistency more than any single post. A missed week, an unanswered comment, or a voice that changes from post to post all chip away at trust before a customer ever reaches out. Staying present every day, in a voice that's unmistakably yours, is the actual job, and it's a hard one to do alone.
