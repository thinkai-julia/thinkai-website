Automation between the conversations

The conversation is only one part of the sales process.

The work that happens between customer conversations is where a large amount of sales momentum is lost.

  • After form submission → create lead + notify team
  • After call → update CRM + create summary
  • No reply → follow-up sequence
  • Appointment booked → confirmations and reminders
  • Appointment missed → recovery workflow
  • Proposal sent → follow-up task
  • Not ready → nurture
  • Old lead → reactivation
