Why this matters

Every missed step costs more than the task itself.

Most operational breakdowns aren't dramatic, they're quiet: an approval that sits for three days, a contract sent with the wrong version attached, a handoff between team members where nothing was written down. Each one chips away at client trust and eats hours no one budgeted for. A business built on clear systems catches these before they become a problem.
