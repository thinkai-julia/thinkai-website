The real problem

Most marketing problems start before the campaign.

Ad spend and content without a strategy behind them are just expensive guesses. These are the issues a roadmap catches before budget gets spent finding them the hard way.

Wrong audience

Budget spent reaching people who were never going to buy.

Weak positioning

Nothing a competitor couldn't credibly claim too.

Disconnected channels

Every channel pulling in a different direction, no plan tying them together.

No offer hierarchy

Every message competing for attention instead of building toward one.

No definition of "qualified"

Sales and marketing arguing about what a good lead even looks like.

No shared priorities

Teams executing quickly, in directions that don't add up.
